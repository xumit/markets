About the data

Daily breadth
—
Source
Daily closes of every S&P 500 member, adjusted for splits and dividends; index level from the S&P 500

Daily breadth counts today's S&P 500 members that closed up or down on the session (a stock that closed exactly unchanged is in neither column), and the share closing above its own simple 20, 50 and 200-day moving average. Today's membership is applied to the whole history, which flatters the past slightly, because stocks dropped from the index are usually ones that fell. The page refreshes automatically after each US trading session.

This describes market conditions only. It is not investment advice or a reason to buy or sell anything.
