Daily breadth counts today's Nifty 500 members that closed up or down on the session (a stock that closed exactly unchanged is in neither column), and the share closing above its own simple 20, 50 and 200-day moving average. Today's membership is applied to the whole history, which flatters the past slightly, because stocks dropped from the index are usually ones that fell.

The distribution uses fixed bands so readings stay comparable from week to week. Drawdown is measured from each stock's own 52-week high, not from an index high, so a name that never joined a rally still shows its own damage. All prices are adjusted for splits, bonuses and demergers. This describes market conditions only. It is not investment advice or a reason to buy or sell anything.
