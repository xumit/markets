Statistics by month

  • Each return is the month-end gold price divided by the prior month-end price, minus one. Prices are in USD per troy ounce. Gold pays no income, so there is nothing to add back.
  • Prices from January 1994 come from TradingView (TVC:GOLD). Earlier months come from the original workbook. The January 1994 return joins the two sources, so it is an approximation.
  • The current month is left out until it ends, so statistics use complete months only.
  • SD is the population standard deviation. P (+) is the share of months with a return above zero. Range H and L are the mean plus and minus one SD.
  • Election Years are years divisible by 4 (1968, 1972, …). Post-Election Years are one year later (1969, 1973, …). Midterm Years are two years later (1970, 1974, …). Pre-Election Years are three years later (1971, 1975, …), and the year before each election.
  • Historical statistics for information only. Past performance is not a reliable guide to future returns. Not investment advice.
