Statistics by month

  • Each return is the month-end close divided by the prior month-end close, minus one.
  • 2010 shows August to December only. November 2026 is excluded because the price data ends on 1 November.
  • SD is the population standard deviation. P (+) is the share of months with a return above zero. Range H and L are the mean plus and minus one SD.
  • Election Years: 2012, 2016, 2020, 2024. Post-Election Years: 2013, 2017, 2021, 2025. Midterm Years: 2014, 2018, 2022, 2026. Pre-Election Years: 2011, 2015, 2019, 2023.
  • Historical statistics for information only. Past performance is not a reliable guide to future returns. Not investment advice.
