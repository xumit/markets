Statistics by month

  • Each return is the month-end index level divided by the prior month-end level, minus one. The index is the S&P 500 price index, so dividends are not included.
  • The current month is left out until it ends, so statistics use complete months only.
  • SD is the population standard deviation. P (+) is the share of months with a return above zero. Range H and L are the mean plus and minus one SD.
  • Election Years are years divisible by 4 (1928, 1932, …). Post-Election Years are one year later (1929, 1933, …). Midterm Years are two years later (1930, 1934, …). Pre-Election Years are three years later (1927, 1931, …), and the year before each election.
  • Historical statistics for information only. Past performance is not a reliable guide to future returns. Not investment advice.
