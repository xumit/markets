Bitcoin Bear Cycles

Each bear cycle from its peak close, rebased to 1.00 on its first day. Prices in USD. Data through 4 November 2026.

Each line is one bear cycle. Click a cycle name to hide or show its line. All five are shown by default. The Average line is the mean of whichever cycles are running on each day.
