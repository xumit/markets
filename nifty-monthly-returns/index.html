Statistics by month

  • Each return is the month-end Nifty 50 price index divided by the prior month-end level, minus one. The index is the price index, so dividends are not included.
  • January 1999 uses the December 1998 close of 884.25 as its starting level.
  • Prices from January 1994 come from TradingView (TVC:GOLD). Earlier months come from the original workbook. The January 1994 return joins the two sources, so it is an approximation.
  • The current month is left out until it ends, so statistics use complete months only.
  • SD is the population standard deviation. P (+) is the share of months with a return above zero. Range H and L are the mean plus and minus one SD.
  • Five-year cycle keyed to Lok Sabha elections (1999, 2004, 2009, 2014, 2019, 2024). Election Year: 1999, 2004, 2009, 2014, 2019, 2024. Post-election Year: one year after. Mid-term Year: two years after. 3rd Year: three years after. Pre-election Year: four years after, the year before each election.
  • Historical statistics for information only. Past performance is not a reliable guide to future returns. Not investment advice.
